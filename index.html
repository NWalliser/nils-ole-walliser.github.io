I am a lecturer (maître de conférences) at the University of Montpellier and a member of the Laboratoire Charles Coulomb .

Research

My research focuses on theoretical biophysics, particularly on modelling cellular and subcellular processes within the framework of non-equilibrium and stochastic thermodynamics, with applications to bacterial motility.

My former research field was high-energy physics and string theory.

Latest news

Our recent work examines how cooperativity shapes adsorption dynamics on a one-dimensional substrate, with applications to bacterial flagellar motor assembly. Read the article in Scientific Reports .
